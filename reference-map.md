# Reference map: dadengroup.in

Source inspected: https://dadengroup.in/

## Visible primary navigation
The reference site uses a compact top navigation with the brand mark followed by Services, Portfolio, Case Studies, Blog, About, ROI Calculator, Contact, and a high-emphasis Free Audit CTA.

## Homepage architecture
1. Hero positioning: a short category eyebrow, oversized split headline, supporting paragraph, two CTAs, and a small positioning statement.
2. Proof band: a dark metric strip with four numbers.
3. Trusted-by row: a restrained logo/name marquee.
4. Services section: two high-level service engines with grouped service links.
5. Differentiators section: four concise reasons to believe.
6. Featured case study: one large proof story with industry, problem, solution, CTA, and result metrics beside imagery.
7. Process section: four numbered stages — Audit, Design, Build, Compound.
8. Client proof section: testimonial cards.
9. Field notes/blog section: article cards with category, title, summary, and read time.
10. FAQ section: accordion questions.
11. Contact conversion section: form with name, work email, company, website, service interest, budget, and message.
12. Footer: service links, company links, legal links, email, and social links.

## Visual cues to translate
The reference is minimal, bright, and editorial: white background, faint grid texture, black type, cobalt blue accent blocks, compact uppercase labels, large tight headlines, thin rules, and deliberate whitespace. The interface uses a slim header and broad section rhythm instead of a dense application sidebar.

## AgencyOS adaptation direction
Keep the reference's top-level marketing-style information architecture for the public-facing layer, but translate each section into AgencyOS language: “What we manage” for services, “Active accounts” for portfolio, “Campaign stories” for case studies, “Operating notes” for blog, “Agency fit” for about, “Retainer calculator” for ROI calculator, and “Start a workspace” for contact/free audit. The authenticated operating desk can remain available as the product workspace behind the public shell.
